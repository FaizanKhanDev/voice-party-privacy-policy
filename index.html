Section 03

Audio Privacy & Microphone Guarantees

🎙️
STRICT AUDIO POLICY DISCLOSURE:
Live voice streaming is powered by WebRTC (LiveKit). Voice audio passes ephemerally through volatile RAM to enable real-time communication. We DO NOT record, store, archive, or sell live voice conversations. Microphone data is never used for advertising or marketing.
Section 04 & 05

How We Use Data & Mini-Games

Data is used strictly to run voice rooms, update CP intimacy meters, process atomic coin debits for gifts, power embedded webview games (Greedy Animal Wheel, Lucky 77, Food Wheel Party), and prevent fraud.

Section 06 & 07

Data Sharing & Third-Party SDKs

We do not sell your data. Trusted service providers include:

  • LiveKit: WebRTC voice streaming infrastructure.
  • Supabase: Database management, auth, and CDC real-time mapping.
  • Google Sign-In: OAuth 2.0 authentication.
  • Expo & Railway: Push notifications & cloud hosting.
Google Play Account Deletion Policy

Account & Data Deletion Instructions

In full compliance with Google Play Developer Policy, users can request complete account and data deletion at any time:

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open VoiceParty App → Go to Profile → Settings → Account & Security.
  2. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Method 2: Web & Email Deletion Request (No App Required)

Send an email to privacy@voiceparty.com or support@voiceparty.com with the subject "Google Play Account Deletion Request" along with your registered email and username. Your account, profile, photos, and chat history will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
